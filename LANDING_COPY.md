## FAQ

### Does this connect to my billing systems?

No. The first version uses manual inputs so founders and engineering managers can audit spend without granting billing access. The goal is to get to a useful estimate quickly, not to create another procurement integration project.

### Is company data shared in public reports?

No. Shared audit URLs remove company name and email. The public report only shows the tool stack, spend totals, estimated savings, and recommendations needed for the report to be useful.

### Are the savings guaranteed?

No. The app uses rule-based recommendations from researched public pricing. Teams should verify contract terms, annual billing, taxes, usage, and enterprise commitments before changing plans.

### What happens if savings exceed $500/month?

The report surfaces a Credex consultation CTA because that level of savings is large enough to justify a deeper review. Credex may be able to help teams capture savings through discounted AI infrastructure credits.

### Why ask for email after the audit?

The tool shows value first. Email capture is only used to save or follow up on the report, especially when the savings opportunity is large enough for a Credex review.
