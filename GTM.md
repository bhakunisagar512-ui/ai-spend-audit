# Go-To-Market

## Target Persona

Small engineering-led teams with 5-75 employees that have adopted multiple AI tools without a procurement process.

Primary buyer:
- Founder, CTO, engineering manager, or operations lead
- Owns software/tooling budget
- Has at least 3 paid AI subscriptions across the team
- Feels AI tools are useful but suspects duplicate spend

## Pain

Teams adopt ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, v0, Gemini, and Windsurf one person at a time. After a few months, nobody knows which seats are active, which tiers are too high, or whether multiple tools are solving the same job.

## Zero-Dollar Acquisition

- Post teardown examples in founder and developer communities
- Offer free audits to 10 small teams in exchange for permission to anonymize learnings
- Share before/after AI-tool-stack screenshots on LinkedIn
- Comment on posts about SaaS cost cutting, developer tooling budgets, and AI adoption
- DM founders who mention Cursor, ChatGPT Team, or Copilot rollouts

## Communities

- Indie Hackers
- r/SaaS
- r/startups
- r/webdev
- r/ExperiencedDevs
- LinkedIn founder/operator posts
- Local startup Discord/WhatsApp groups

## Message

Most teams do not need fewer AI tools. They need fewer wasted seats and the right plans for the people actually using them.

## First 10 Users

1. Ask developer friends to run the audit.
2. Review their results manually.
3. Turn repeated savings patterns into product rules.
4. Ask each user for one referral to another founder or engineering lead.
