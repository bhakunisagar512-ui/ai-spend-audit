# Go-To-Market

The exact target user is a founder, CTO, or engineering manager at a 5-75 person seed or Series A startup where AI tools were adopted bottom-up. They have Cursor or Copilot in engineering, ChatGPT or Claude for general work, maybe API spend for product features, and no procurement owner. They are not searching for "AI spend management" yet. They are scrolling X posts about runway, looking at a scary Vercel/OpenAI/Anthropic invoice, reading Hacker News comments about Cursor pricing, or asking in a founder group whether ChatGPT Team is worth it.

Specific channels:
- `r/SaaS`, `r/startups`, `r/webdev`, and `r/ExperiencedDevs`
- Indie Hackers product feedback threads
- X searches for "Cursor bill", "OpenAI invoice", "Claude Team pricing", "AI tools spend"
- Founder WhatsApp/Discord groups from college and internship networks
- LinkedIn comments on posts about burn reduction and AI tooling
- Hacker News "Show HN" and launch discussion threads

First 100 users with $0 budget:
1. Manually audit 10 founders' stacks and ask permission to anonymize before/after screenshots.
2. Post a short teardown: "This 12-person team was paying $X/mo too much for AI tools."
3. DM founders who mention Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Team, or OpenAI bills with a specific note, not a generic pitch.
4. Share a public audit URL in communities so people see the output before clicking.
5. Ask every completed audit user for one intro to another founder or engineering lead.

Credex's unfair channel is discounted supply. A generic SaaS audit tool can say "downgrade." Credex can say "you are paying retail for something we may source at a discount." That makes the tool more than advice; it is a path to savings.

Week-1 traction if this works:
- 100 completed audits
- 25 captured emails
- 10 audits above $500/month savings
- 5 Credex consultation requests
- 2 serious credit-purchase conversations

The wedge is not "manage all SaaS spend." It is narrower: show AI overspend fast, then route high-savings cases to Credex.
